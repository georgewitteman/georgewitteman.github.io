Microwave time converter

Enter what the box says to get the power level and time to set on your microwave. Type the time like on a microwave: 2, 3, 0 is 2:30.

Food heats about in proportion to wattage × time. Power level 5 runs at about half power, so a level below 10 only helps when the box expects a weaker microwave than yours or asks for less than full power.
